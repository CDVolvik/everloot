# Roadmap

What we are working on, and what you can pick up. The live board is the
[Everloot project](https://github.com/Everloot/everloot/projects) on GitHub; issues labelled
`good first issue` and `help wanted` are open for anyone.

## Now: launch (WoW: Forever, 2026-11-04)

- The addon on CurseForge, checked in the live client.
- Everloot Desktop App: a first `desktop-v0.1.0` release, tried on Windows with the live client.
- Enough uploads at launch that the first drops, vendors and prices reach three agreeing installs.

## Next

- An open data export: the published data as files anyone can download and build on, under
  CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Sharing data with [Polestar](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar). Its guides could use observed
  Forever data, and its players could add to ours.
- A newer lore archive, if the Warcraft Wiki allows a current dump.

## Later

- **Mobile apps.** An Android app on Google Play first, then iOS on the App Store: the database, the talent
  calculator and the raid builder in your pocket, and uploads from the desktop companion without a PC
  nearby. Free like the site; an account only adds sync. Code will live in its own repositories
  (`Everloot/android`, `Everloot/ios`).
- Recipe windows from real crafts, auction deposits, and the gaps listed in
  [`docs/sources/source-catalog.md`](docs/sources/source-catalog.md).
