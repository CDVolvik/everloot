## Overview

Blackwing Lair is the top of Blackrock Spire, and it belongs to Nefarian, son of Deathwing. He lives there in
the guise of Lord Victor Nefarius and rules over the Blackrock orcs and the black dragonflight that serve him.

Nefarian is not just holding ground. He is trying to make something new: the chromatic dragonflight, dragons
bred to carry the strengths of every flight at once. His lair is full of the results, along with the dragons and
dragonkin who guard his labs and the captives he has broken. Players fight up through his halls to reach him.

## Getting there

The lair is inside Blackrock Mountain, at the summit of Blackrock Spire, in {zone:1428}. The game files list
Morgan's Vigil and Flame Crest in Burning Steppes, and Thorium Point in Searing Gorge, as the flight points
around the mountain.

In the original game players first had to fight through Upper Blackrock Spire and defeat General Drakkisath
before they could enter the lair.

## History

Deathwing left his children to carry on his plans while he was away. Nefarian made Blackrock Spire his seat
and the Blackrock orcs, led by Warchief Rend Blackhand, his army. From there he fights a long war for the
mountain against Ragnaros and the Dark Irons below, in [[molten-core]] and Blackrock Depths.

While he built his army, his sister Onyxia worked on Stormwind from the inside. Her part of the story is told in
[[onyxias-lair]].

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names the first raids that open in WoW: Forever: the Barrow Deeps,
Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair. Blackwing Lair is not among them, and Blizzard has not said when it will open.
The entrance is in the current beta build's game files, so the bosses, loot and quests on this page fill in from
those files and from what players record once they play.
