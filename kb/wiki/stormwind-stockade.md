## Overview

The Stockade is Stormwind's prison. Its inmates have broken out of their cells and hold the whole block, and
the city guard has fallen back to the gate. Many of the prisoners belong to the Defias Brotherhood, the same
gang behind the trouble in Westfall, and the longer players spend inside, the less the riot looks like an
accident.

It is a small dungeon, a single block of corridors and cells. Because it sits inside an Alliance capital,
almost everyone who runs it is Alliance, and many come in between other quests while they are in the city
anyway.

## Getting there

The entrance is inside {zone:1453} itself, so there is no road to travel. The game files put Stormwind's own
flight master closest to it, and players arriving by tram from Ironforge are already in the city. The
warden stands by the prison gate and is the person to speak to before going in.

## History

Warden Thelwater runs the prison and asks players to put the uprising down. He suspects the riot was planned
rather than sudden, and his suspicion falls on a Defias prisoner, Bazil Thredd. That thread connects the
Stockade to [[deadmines]]: the letter found on Edwin VanCleef leads Alliance players back to Stormwind, and
from there to the question of what Thredd knows and who he has been talking to.

Other prisoners are wanted elsewhere. Quest givers in Duskwood and the Wetlands each have a grudge against
someone locked up here and want that inmate stopped for good while the cells are open. Taken together, the
Stockade's quests are less about the prison itself than about the people Stormwind put in it, and about how
far the Defias reach into the city.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard has not announced anything specific to the Stockade for WoW: Forever yet. The entrance is in the
current beta build's game files. The bosses, loot and quests on this page fill in from those files and from
