## Overview

The Temple of Ahn'Qiraj is the center of the Qiraji empire, deep under the city in the south of Silithus. The
Qiraji obey the Twin Emperors, Vek'lor and Vek'nilash, and the emperors in turn serve something far older. In
the deepest chamber lies C'Thun, one of the Old Gods, and the whole insect empire above exists to carry out its
will.

The temple is a long descent through Qiraji royalty, giant silithid and stranger things toward the emperors and
then to C'Thun itself. The Brood of Nozdormu, the bronze dragonflight, keeps watch over the fight and rewards
those who take it on.

## Getting there

The temple is in {zone:1451}, behind the Scarab Wall at the southern edge of the zone, past the same gates as the
[[ruins-of-ahn-qiraj]]. The game files list Cenarion Hold as the flight point in Silithus.

In the original game the gates stayed closed until each realm finished its war effort and a player rang the
Scarab Gong with the Scepter of the Shifting Sands.

## History

The Qiraji are servants of the Old God beneath them. C'Thun shaped the insect races into an army, and in the
War of the Shifting Sands that army pushed deep into Kalimdor before the night elves and the dragonflights
sealed it behind the Scarab Wall.

The bronze dragon Anachronos helped lead that defence, which is why the bronze flight still watches the temple.
The emperors were never defeated, only shut in, and C'Thun was left waiting underneath.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names the first raids that open in WoW: Forever: the Barrow Deeps,
Hyjal Summit and [[onyxias-lair]]. Ahn'Qiraj Temple is not among them, and Blizzard has not said when it will
open or whether the gates open the same way. The entrance is in the current beta build's game files, so the
bosses and loot on this page fill in from those files and from what players record.
