## Overview

Razorfen Kraul is a warren of tunnels and ledges grown inside enormous bramble vines at the southern end of
the Barrens. It is one of the great strongholds of the quilboar, the boar-like people who fill the Barrens
with their camps, and its leader is Charlga Razorflank, the crone of the Kraul. Inside, quilboar warriors and
mystics share the thorns with their trained boars and the beasts that live among the vines.

The paths twist up, down and around the brambles, and it is easy to lose track of which way is out. Players
from both factions come here, which is unusual for a dungeon so deep in Horde territory.

## Getting there

The entrance is in the far south of {zone:1413}, close to where the Barrens drop away into Thousand Needles.
The closest flight points in the game files are Thalanaar in Feralas and Freewind Post in Thousand Needles.
Horde players coming down the Barrens' main road from the north can also make the walk on foot.

## History

The quilboar believe the thorns grew from the body of Agamaggan, a great boar demigod who fell long ago
fighting the demons of the Burning Legion. The brambles are sacred to them, and they build their homes deep
in the coils. The Kraul is one of two such warrens; the other, Razorfen Downs, lies nearby and is a separate
dungeon.

Quest givers on both sides want the Kraul's strength broken and Charlga stopped. A goblin in Ratchet pays
for blueleaf tubers dug up from the dungeon's floor, and a goblin trader the quilboar have taken prisoner
asks to be led back out alive. Between them, these quests give most groups a reason to see the Kraul from
its entrance to its deepest chamber.
