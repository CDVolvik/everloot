## Overview

Ogre raiders have been working the roads and villages of the Riverglades, carrying off travelers and wrecking
whatever settlements they pass through. Blizzard sends players after them, following their trail back to
Krol'dok Stronghold, the fortress they come from.

From Blizzard's description, it is a large place rather than a single hall: workshops, camps spread across the
grounds and guarded passages inside the walls. The goal is to stop the raids at the source. There is a second
thread too. Blizzard mentions odd chanting coming from the stronghold after dark, and leaves it there as
something for players to look into.

This page does not name the ogres' leaders or say what the chanting is. Blizzard's announcement does not, and
nothing has been recorded in play yet.

## Getting there

Blizzard places the stronghold in the Riverglades and lists it with the Eastern Kingdoms dungeons. The
Riverglades is new in WoW: Forever. It is in the game files, but Everloot's map holds it back for now and the
wiki has nothing on the zone yet, so this page cannot point to a flight point or a road. That will change once
Blizzard says more about the zone or players record their way there.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for Krol'dok Stronghold. That is why this page
is not tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows
are missing here.

## In WoW: Forever

Krol'dok Stronghold is new in WoW: Forever, in a zone that is new as well. This page is written from Blizzard's
announcement of the new dungeons on October 20, 2026, and nothing on it has been seen in play or recorded on
Everloot yet. When the entrance turns up in the game files or players record a run, its entrance is bound here and the
bosses, loot and level range fill in.
