## Overview

Hyjal Summit takes a raid to the top of Mount Hyjal, close to Nordrassil, the World Tree of the night elves.
Blizzard says it opens with a fight against an ancient guardian. After that the raid is not a straight line:
groups pick from several paths across the mountain, through ruins both on the surface and underground, as they
take on the forces threatening Hyjal and clear the way to the last fights.

Blizzard does not say who or what those forces are, and this page does not guess. The raid's size and bosses
will show here from the game files, not from this text.

## Getting there

Blizzard places the raid at the summit of Mount Hyjal, near Nordrassil, in the north of Kalimdor. Mount Hyjal is
in the beta's game files, but Everloot's map holds it back for now, and the game files Everloot has read do not
have an entrance for Hyjal Summit. That is why this page is not tied to an entrance or a zone yet. The
[[barrow-deeps]] lie under the same mountain.

## History

The night elves planted Nordrassil on the summit after the War of the Ancients, and the tree bound them to the
land and kept them from ageing. In the Third War the demon lord Archimonde led the Burning Legion up the mountain
to destroy it. Night elves, humans and orcs held him off long enough for Malfurion Stormrage to spring a trap,
and Archimonde was destroyed at the World Tree. The night elves lost their immortality in the victory.

## In WoW: Forever

Hyjal Summit is new in WoW: Forever. Blizzard's raid overview, published on October 20, 2026, names it as one of
the first raids, opening on December 9 alongside the Barrow Deeps and [[onyxias-lair]]. Blizzard currently plans
for raids to reset weekly. Nothing on this page has been seen in play or recorded on Everloot yet; when the
entrance turns up in the game files, it is bound here and the bosses and loot fill in.
