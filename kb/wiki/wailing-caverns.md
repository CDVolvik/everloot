## Overview

The Wailing Caverns are a web of caves beneath the Barrens, named for the moaning sound the wind makes as it
moves through cracks in the rock. A night elf druid named Naralex came here hoping to use the Emerald Dream
to bring life back to the dry plains above. Something went wrong while he slept: the dream became a nightmare
he cannot wake from, and its pull has twisted the caves around him. Animals have turned into strange deviate
creatures, and his own followers now serve the nightmare instead of him.

The caves are close to the Crossroads, and for many Horde players they are the first real dungeon in
Kalimdor. The tunnels loop and double back on each other, so a group that comes in without a plan can spend a
long time lost.

## Getting there

The entrance lies in {zone:1413}, in a hollow in the hills southwest of the Crossroads. The Crossroads flight
point is the closest one in the game files. A few
people camped in the cave mouth above the dungeon offer work to anyone who passes, whichever side they fight
for.

## History

Naralex's students called themselves the Druids of the Fang. Lord Cobrahn, Lady Anacondra, Lord Pythas and
Lord Serpentis were meant to guard their master's sleep, but the nightmare reached them too, and they now
lead the cave's corrupted druids and serpents. One disciple stayed loyal. He waits near the entrance, and if
a group clears the way for him, he tries to wake Naralex. That attempt draws out Mutanus the Devourer, the
creature at the heart of the nightmare.

Quest givers in Thunder Bluff and around the Barrens send players to put down the Druids of the Fang and to
bring back samples of the caves' strange growth.
