## Overview

The Barrow Deeps is a prison buried under Kalimdor, inside Mount Hyjal. For ten thousand years it held
Illidan Stormrage. Blizzard says its dark passages have now been opened, and raiders go down into them to find
out what happened to those left behind there after Illidan was gone, and how the wardens
mean to keep whatever is still down there from getting out.

It is a raid for characters at the level cap, and it is meant for a smaller group than Onyxia's Lair. The
raid's size and its bosses will show here from the game files, not from this text.

## Getting there

Blizzard says the prison has more than one way in. Entrances connect to it through Mount Hyjal, through
{zone:1452} and through [[blackmaw-hold]], the new furbolg dungeon in Azshara, so raiders can choose their
path down.

Mount Hyjal is in the beta's game files, but Everloot's map holds it back for now, and the game files Everloot
has read do not have an entrance for the Barrow Deeps. That is why this page is not tied to an entrance or a
zone yet.

## History

Illidan Stormrage was locked away by his own brother, Malfurion, after the War of the Ancients, for making a
new Well of Eternity on Hyjal. The night elves kept him under the earth for millennia, watched by wardens, until
Tyrande Whisperwind set him free during the Third War to fight the Scourge. The prison stayed behind him, and
Blizzard's story for the raid starts with the people it left there.

## In WoW: Forever

The Barrow Deeps is new in WoW: Forever as a raid. Blizzard's raid overview, published on October 20, 2026,
names it as one of the first raids, opening on December 9 alongside [[hyjal-summit]] and [[onyxias-lair]].
Blizzard currently plans for raids to reset weekly. Nothing on this page has been seen in play or recorded on
Everloot yet; when the entrance turns up in the game files, it is bound here and the bosses and loot fill in.
