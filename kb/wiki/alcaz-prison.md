## Overview

Alcaz Prison is the long-shut prison on Alcaz Island, and in WoW: Forever its doors open. Blizzard says the
Defias Brotherhood and the naga are already inside and at war with each other, and a group walks into the
middle of that. Past them is the prison itself and whoever lives in it. Blizzard adds that it holds new clues
to "a familiar mystery", without saying which one.

## Getting there

Alcaz Island lies off the coast of {zone:1445}. The game files list Theramore as the zone's Alliance flight point
and Brackenwall Village as the Horde one. Blizzard has not said how players cross to the island or where on it
the entrance is.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for Alcaz Prison. That is why this page is not
tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows are
missing here.

## History

For as long as players have looked out at Alcaz Island, there have been stories about the prison on it and the
people it might hold. The best known is that King Varian Wrynn of Stormwind, who vanished while his young son
Anduin sat on the throne, was locked up there. The prison itself stayed shut. Blizzard stops short of saying
whether Varian is actually inside, and this page does the same.

## In WoW: Forever

Alcaz Prison is new in WoW: Forever. In the original game the island was there, but its prison was never open
to players. This page is written from Blizzard's announcement of the new dungeons on October 20, 2026,
and nothing on it has been seen in play or recorded on Everloot yet. When the entrance turns up in the game files
or players record a run, its entrance is bound here and the
bosses, loot and level range fill in.
