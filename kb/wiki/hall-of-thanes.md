## Overview

The Hall of Thanes is the place under Ironforge where the dwarves lay their kings to rest. The tombs are kept
with care, and Blizzard describes the halls as quiet except for the voices of the dead that still seem to
move through the stone.

That quiet has been broken. Someone has forced their way into the hall, and the dwarves think it was the Dark
Iron clan, their old enemies from Blackrock Mountain. A group goes down to throw the intruders out, and then to
find out what the break-in has disturbed among the tombs. Blizzard has not said more than that about the story,
and this page will not guess.

Of the new dungeons Blizzard has announced for WoW: Forever, this is the one it lists for the lowest levels.

## Getting there

Blizzard puts the entrance beneath Ironforge, in {zone:1426}. Alliance players can walk to it from inside their
own capital. Horde groups have to travel deep into Alliance territory to reach it, which is a long and
unfriendly road at the levels the dungeon is built for.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for the Hall of Thanes. That is why this page
is not tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows
are missing here.

## In WoW: Forever

The Hall of Thanes is new in WoW: Forever; the original game had no dungeon under Ironforge. Everything on this
page comes from Blizzard's announcement of the new dungeons, published on October 20, 2026. None of it has been
seen in play or recorded by players on Everloot yet. Once the entrance shows up in the game files, or players
record a run, this page becomes a full dungeon page and the numbers fill in by themselves.
