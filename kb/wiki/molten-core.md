## Overview

Molten Core lies at the very bottom of Blackrock Mountain, below the Dark Iron city of Blackrock Depths. It is
a cavern of lava and broken rock, and it belongs to Ragnaros the Firelord, one of the elemental lords. His
servants fill it: fire elementals, molten giants, core hounds and the lieutenants who answer to him, led by his
steward, Majordomo Executus.

The raid is a long walk down through the Firelord's lieutenants before the steward will call his master up out
of the lava. Fire is the theme from start to finish, and groups in the original game learned to gather fire
resistance gear for it.

## Getting there

Blackrock Mountain stands on the border between {zone:1427} and {zone:1428}, and the way into Molten Core goes
through it, down past the Dark Iron halls. The game files list Thorium Point in Searing Gorge, and Morgan's Vigil
and Flame Crest in Burning Steppes, as the flight points around the mountain.

## History

Long ago the Dark Iron dwarves lost a war against the other dwarf clans. Their sorcerer-thane, Thaurissan, tried
to turn it around by summoning a powerful elemental to fight for him. What answered was Ragnaros. His arrival
tore the land apart, raised Blackrock Mountain and left the scorched country around it. The Dark Irons became his
slaves, and their descendants still serve him in the depths.

The Hydraxian Waterlords, water elementals and old enemies of Ragnaros, take an interest in the core as well.
Their leader, Duke Hydraxis, asks adventurers to put out the runes of fire that the Firelord's lieutenants guard.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names the first raids that open in WoW: Forever: the Barrow Deeps,
Hyjal Summit and [[onyxias-lair]]. Molten Core is not among them, and Blizzard has not said when it will open.
The entrance is in the current beta build's game files, so the bosses, loot and quests on this page fill in from
those files and from what players record once they play. The raid sits next to [[blackwing-lair]] in the same
mountain.
