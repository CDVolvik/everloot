## Overview

Zul'Gurub was the capital of the Gurubashi trolls, a jungle city of temples and stepped stone built in the
north-east of Stranglethorn Vale. It has been taken back by the worst of its old gods. Hakkar the Soulflayer,
the blood god, has returned, and the city's high priests serve him.

Each high priest has taken on the power of one of the troll animal gods, the bat, the snake, the spider, the
tiger and others, and Hakkar draws strength from them. A raid works through the priests and the city's other
masters before facing the Soulflayer himself.

## Getting there

The city is in {zone:1434}, in the north-east of the jungle. The game files list Grom'gol on the west coast and
Booty Bay at the southern tip as the zone's flight points.

The Zandalar tribe, the trolls who want Hakkar stopped, set up camp on Yojamba Isle off the Stranglethorn
coast. In the original game that is where players picked up their quests and earned standing with them.

## History

Long ago the Gurubashi worshipped Hakkar and paid him in blood. He wanted more than they could give, and in
the end the trolls turned on him and drove him out of the world. The priests who still served him, the Atal'ai,
were exiled into the Swamp of Sorrows, where their temple later sank into the marsh.

The Atal'ai never stopped trying to bring their god back. Their work finally pays off in Zul'Gurub, and the
Zandalari, the oldest of the troll tribes, send their own people to Yojamba to gather help against him.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names the first raids that open in WoW: Forever: the Barrow Deeps,
Hyjal Summit and [[onyxias-lair]]. Zul'Gurub is not among them, and Blizzard has not said when it will open. The
entrance is in the current beta build's game files, so the bosses, loot and quests on this page fill in from
those files and from what players record once they play.
