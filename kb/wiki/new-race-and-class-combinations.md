## What changed

In the original game, the race a player picked ruled out a good part of the class list. WoW: Forever loosens
that. Blizzard's race and class article, published on September 22, 2026, names the pairings that are new:

- Orc Mage
- Troll Warlock
- Undead Paladin
- Human Hunter
- Gnome Priest
- Dwarf Shaman

On top of those, the new [[skyborne]] race is playable on both sides with a different class list on each. Horde
Skyborne, the Windshapers, can be Shaman; Alliance Skyborne, the High Order, can be Mages. Both can be Druids,
Hunters, Rogues and Warriors.

The same article says some racial traits were adjusted for WoW: Forever, so that they suit the wider class
choices and give each race a clearer identity. It lists every race's traits but does not mark which ones
changed, so this page does not either.

## What it affects

The biggest shift is in the two classes that used to belong to one faction. In the original game Paladins were
Alliance only and Shaman were Horde only. In WoW: Forever the Horde can play Paladins through the Undead, and
the Alliance can play Shaman through the Dwarves. Both factions now have both classes to bring to groups and
raids.

The rest of the list mostly widens choice for players who wanted a class without the race that used to come
with it: a Human who wants to be a Hunter no longer has to be a Dwarf or a Night Elf, and an Orc who wants to
cast spells is no longer limited to Shaman and Warlock.

Everloot's WoW Forever page has the full race and class table for both factions, copied from Blizzard's article,
with the new pairings marked. The census counts how many uploaded characters play each combination, so it will
show which of the new ones players actually take up.
