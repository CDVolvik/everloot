## Overview

Onyxia is a black dragon, a daughter of Deathwing and the younger sister of Nefarian. Her lair is a cave in the
swamps of Dustwallow Marsh, where she sits on her hoard and raises her whelps. The raid is a single long fight
against her, with her brood joining in, and her fire is what groups prepare for.

Onyxia does not only wait in her cave. She has a human disguise, Lady Katrana Prestor, and in it she sits close
to the throne of Stormwind and steers the kingdom's nobles toward her own ends.

## Getting there

The lair is in {zone:1445}, in the dragon-infested south of the marsh. The game files list Theramore and
Brackenwall Village as the zone's flight points, for the Alliance and the Horde.

Nobody gets in without the {item:16309}, and each faction earns it its own way. The Alliance chain starts in
the Burning Steppes and runs through Blackrock Depths and a confrontation in Stormwind. The Horde chain starts in
the Badlands, takes players through Lower Blackrock Spire and on to Rexxar in Desolace and the drake Emberstrife
in Dustwallow. Both end in Upper Blackrock Spire with General Drakkisath.

## History

Onyxia and Nefarian took over their father's work in the Eastern Kingdoms after Deathwing went missing. Nefarian
took Blackrock Spire. Onyxia went to work on Stormwind from the inside. While King Varian was missing she used
her disguise to keep the court divided, and the Alliance attunement quests are built around catching her at it.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names Onyxia's Lair as one of the first raids in WoW: Forever,
opening on December 9 with the Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit. Blizzard's own advice is to build fire resistance
before going in and to be ready to move as the fight changes. The amulet only has to be in a player's bags, not
worn. Blizzard currently plans for raids to reset weekly. The entrance is in the current beta build's game files;
the fight and its loot fill in from those files and from what players record.
