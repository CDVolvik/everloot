## Overview

An old troll city that sank long ago has come back up out of the ocean off Stranglethorn. Its streets are open
to the air for the first time in a very long while, and Blizzard says trolls and naga have both moved in. A
pirate ship nearby seems to have come off badly in whatever happened.

The Drowned City is an open-air dungeon, and Blizzard says the weather is part of it. It starts in mist, rain
sets in as a group goes deeper, and by the last fight a full storm has built up overhead.

Blizzard has not said who the naga serve or who waits at the end.

## Getting there

Blizzard places the city off the coast of {zone:1434}, west of Grom'gol. The entrance is under the water, so
expect to dive to find it.

Grom'gol, the landmark Blizzard uses, is the zone's Horde flight point in the game files. The Alliance flight
point in the zone is Booty Bay, at the far southern tip. Blizzard has not said exactly where off the coast the
city rises or how far out the dive is.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for the Drowned City. That is why this page is
not tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows are
missing here.

## History

Stranglethorn was the heart of the Gurubashi troll empire, whose old capital, [[zul-gurub]], still stands in
the north of the jungle. Blizzard calls the risen city ancient and troll-built but has not said which trolls
built it or whether it belonged to the Gurubashi, so this page does not say either.

## In WoW: Forever

The Drowned City is new in WoW: Forever. This page is written from Blizzard's announcement of the new dungeons
on October 20, 2026, and nothing on it has been seen in play or recorded on Everloot yet. When the entrance
turns up in the game files or players record a run, its entrance is bound here and the
bosses, loot and level range fill in.
