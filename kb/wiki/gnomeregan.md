## Overview

Gnomeregan was the gnomes' home, a city of workshops and machinery built into the mountains of Dun Morogh. It
fell from below. Troggs came up out of the deep earth and overran the lower halls, and High Tinker
Mekkatorque, on the advice of his adviser Sicco Thermaplugg, flooded the city with toxic radiation to drive
them out. The plan killed many gnomes along with the troggs. The survivors fled to Ironforge, and
Thermaplugg stayed behind and made the ruins his own kingdom.

The city now holds troggs, irradiated gnomes who never left, Dark Iron dwarves picking over the wreckage, and
a great deal of machinery that still runs without anyone to mind it. It is a long dungeon that winds through
several districts, and groups often need more than one visit to see the end of it.

## Getting there

The front gate is in the west of {zone:1426}, where the gnomes once came and went from their city. The game files put
Ironforge's flight master closest to it, so most players ride to Ironforge and travel west from there.

## History

The gnomes remember Thermaplugg as the man who betrayed them, and he now rules what is left of the city. Mekkatorque leads his people in exile from Tinker Town in Ironforge, and
they have not given up on taking their home back. Quest givers there and around Dun Morogh want players to
recover what was left behind, deal with the worst of the radiation's results, and bring Thermaplugg's rule to
an end.

Horde players hear about the city from goblins, who see the gnomes' abandoned workshops as a prize and want
their own share of what is inside. For them Gnomeregan is less a tragedy than a salvage yard, and they are
happy to pay anyone willing to go in and bring things out.
