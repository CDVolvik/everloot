## Overview

The Skyborne are WoW: Forever's new playable race. They also call themselves the shen'dorei, which Blizzard
translates as "the hidden people". Their story starts thousands of years ago, when a band of exiled Highborne
struck a bargain with the spirits of the wind and built a society in Skywall, the elemental plane of air.

Every Skyborne begins on Zephras Isle, a new starting zone. By the time a player arrives, the wind spirits that
protected the island have gone missing. Without them its old buildings are giving way, its settlements are
drifting apart, and the Skyborne are arguing about what to do.

That disagreement is where the two factions come from. A player picks one at character creation:

- Windshaper Skyborne join the Horde. Their line goes back to the first shen'dorei to receive skysight, the
  wind spirits' gift. They want their vanished elemental teachers found and the spirits restored. They can be
  Shaman.
- High Order Skyborne join the Alliance. Their ancestors were Highborne scholars of Eldre'Thalas, the city now
  known as Dire Maul. To them, losing the spirits showed how dangerous it was to depend on them, so they are
  going after the arcane knowledge their people let slip. They can be Mages.

On both sides a Skyborne can also be a Druid, Hunter, Rogue or Warrior, and their druids get forms made for the
race. Both share racial traits built around wind and the elements, among them a short glide called Walk on Air.
Windshapers add Skysight, a speed boost, and the High Order adds Read Ley Line, which speeds up health and mana
regeneration.

## In WoW: Forever

The Skyborne are new in WoW: Forever; the original game had no such race. On October 7, 2026 Blizzard said it
was reworking the high-definition Skyborne druid forms to sit better beside the rest of the world, and that
standard-definition Skyborne character models, druid forms included, are being made for early 2027. Everloot's WoW
Forever page shows the full race and class table from Blizzard's post.
