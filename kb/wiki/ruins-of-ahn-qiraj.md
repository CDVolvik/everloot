## Overview

The Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj are the outer districts of the Qiraji city in the far south of Silithus. The Qiraji are
insect warriors, and with them come the silithid swarms that have overrun the desert. Inside the walls the
Cenarion Circle has pushed in to break the Qiraji hold, and players go in with them.

The ruins are a wide, open part of the city. They end at Ossirian the Unscarred, the Qiraji general who commands
this part of the defence and is said never to have been wounded.

## Getting there

The city is in {zone:1451}, behind the Scarab Wall at the southern edge of the zone. The game files list
Cenarion Hold as the flight point in Silithus.

In the original game the gates were sealed when the content arrived. They opened only after each realm's war
effort was finished and a player rang the Scarab Gong with the Scepter of the Shifting Sands. The ruins and the
[[ahn-qiraj-temple]] lie behind the same gates.

## History

About a thousand years ago the Qiraji and the silithid poured out of Ahn'Qiraj and attacked the night elves in
what became the War of the Shifting Sands. The night elves could not win alone. The bronze dragon Anachronos and
the other dragonflights helped them drive the Qiraji back and seal them behind the Scarab Wall. Anachronos made
the Scepter of the Shifting Sands to open the wall again if it ever had to be.

Fandral Staghelm, now the archdruid, lost his son Valstann in that war, and the scepter was broken afterwards.
The silithid unrest in Silithus is what brings the Cenarion Circle back to the wall.

## In WoW: Forever

Blizzard's raid overview of October 20, 2026 names the first raids that open in WoW: Forever: the Barrow Deeps,
Hyjal Summit and [[onyxias-lair]]. The Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj are not among them, and Blizzard has not said when they
will open or whether the gates open the same way. The entrance is in the current beta build's game files, so the
bosses and loot on this page fill in from those files and from what players record.
