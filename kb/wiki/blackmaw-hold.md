## Overview

Behind a gate in Azshara that has been sealed for a long time is a great city of the furbolgs, the bear-like
people of Kalimdor's forests. It belongs to the Blackmaw clan, and Blizzard says the Blackmaw are losing a
fight against a corruption that is spreading through their groves and the tunnels that wind through the
mountain.

Players who know Kalimdor have seen this before. In the original game most furbolg tribes had already fallen to
corruption, and the Timbermaw of Felwood and Winterspring were the ones still holding out. Blizzard does not say
what is corrupting the Blackmaw, so this page does not guess.

Blizzard calls Blackmaw Hold a large dungeon for characters at the level cap, with more than one route through
it and several bosses. It also says the deepest part of the hold may lead somewhere even further down.

## Getting there

The hold is in {zone:1447}. The game files list Valormok as the zone's Horde flight point and Talrendis Point as
the Alliance one. Blizzard has not said where in Azshara the sealed gate is.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for Blackmaw Hold. That is why this page is not
tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows are
missing here.

## In WoW: Forever

Blackmaw Hold is new in WoW: Forever. Blizzard's raid overview, also published on October 20, 2026, lists
Blackmaw Hold as one of the ways into the Barrow Deeps, one of the first raids. That fits the hint about a path
further down, though Blizzard does not join the two in so many words.

This page is written from those two announcements, and nothing on it has been seen in play or recorded on
Everloot yet. When the entrance turns up in the game files or players record a run, this becomes a full
dungeon page with live data.
