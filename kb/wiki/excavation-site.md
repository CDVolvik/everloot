## Overview

Something has changed in the hills above Whelgar's Excavation. Blizzard describes a mist rolling down from the
mountains and flashes of light along the skyline. Past the dig site is a place that looks as if time stopped
there, watched over by titan constructs that no longer behave as they should and by wildlife dangerous enough
to need a group.

The dwarves of the Explorers' League dig across Azeroth for traces of the titans, so a find like this is
exactly what they would come running for. Blizzard frames the dungeon around that: the League wants to know
what has woken up past the excavation, and so do the adventurers who go in first.

That is as much as the announcement says. It does not name the place beyond the dig, the constructs or anyone
who waits at the end, and this page leaves those out until they can be checked.

## Getting there

Blizzard gives two descriptions of the location that agree with each other: above Whelgar's Excavation, and
near Thelgen Rock and Dun Algaz, all in {zone:1437}. That puts it in the south of the Wetlands, toward the pass
that leads on to Loch Modan. The game files list Menethil Harbor as the Wetlands' flight point.

The game files Everloot has read so far do not have an entrance for the Excavation Site. That is why this page
is not tied to a dungeon entrance yet, and why the level range, bosses and loot a dungeon page normally shows
are missing here.

## In WoW: Forever

The Excavation Site is new in WoW: Forever. This page is written from Blizzard's announcement of the new
dungeons on October 20, 2026, and nothing on it has been seen in play or recorded on Everloot yet. Once the
entrance shows up in the game files or players record a run, the page becomes a full dungeon page with its
numbers filled in.
