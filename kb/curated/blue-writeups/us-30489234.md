Blizzard is holding a public stress test of WoW: Forever on October 21. The post first gave October 19. Kaivax then edited it and confirmed the new date in a reply on October 16, and the linked news article now carries the same update. The Bluesky post from October 15 still says October 19.

For the test, the Beta goes offline and the game opens on the regional hardware it will run on at launch. Start times from the post:

- EU: October 21, 19:00 CEST
- Americas and Oceania: October 21, 2:00 p.m. PDT
- Taipei: October 22, 5:00 a.m.
- Korea: October 22, 6:00 a.m.

Any WoW account can join, and no subscription is needed. Blizzard wants ordinary play in large numbers: make a character, do some quests, trade, chat and group up. The realms stay open for a few hours. The news article adds that the window may close early or run late.

When the test ends, every character is deleted, names included, and the Beta reopens. Don't put effort into a name you want to keep. Name reservation is a separate event that starts on October 27 ([our notes](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30553826-claim-your-world-of-warcraft-forever-character-names)). End times for each region are in Kaivax's [heads-up post](https://everloot.app/changes/us-30553354-heads-up-wow-forever-stress-test-on-october-21) from October 20.
